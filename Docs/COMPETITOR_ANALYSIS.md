# Tracker Competitor Analysis
## Target Market: Small to Medium Teams & Professional Firms

*Last Updated: January 2026*

---

## Executive Summary

Tracker operates in the 1:1 meeting / team management / performance feedback space, targeting **small-to-medium teams (5-100 people)** and **professional firms**. With PostgreSQL support for shared databases, we now confidently serve the SMB market, not just individual managers.

### Market Position Evolution
- **Previous Target**: Small teams (5-50 people), individual managers
- **Current Target**: Small-to-Medium businesses (5-100 people), multi-user environments
- **Why the upgrade**: PostgreSQL shared database support enables team-wide deployment, professional data handling, and multi-user concurrency

### Tracker's Differentiators
1. **One-time purchase option** - Unique in market (no recurring per-seat costs)
2. **Desktop-first with offline support** - Native Windows app, works without internet
3. **AI features included** - No extra charges for AI insights, suggestions, prep
4. **Local/Shared database flexibility** - SQLite for individuals, PostgreSQL for teams
5. **Privacy-first** - Data stays on your infrastructure, not vendor cloud

### Key Market Changes (2026)
- ⚠️ **Microsoft Viva Goals RETIRED** (Dec 31, 2025) - Major competitive threat removed
- 15Five now charges $2-19/user/mo extra for AI features
- Fellow.ai pivoted to pure AI meeting notes (no goals/reviews)
- AI meeting assistants commoditizing rapidly
