# ProCohere Insights: User Guide

## What Are Insights?

**Insights** are intelligent recommendations that ProCohere automatically generates by analyzing your team's activities, goals, metrics, and interactions. Think of insights as your personal AI assistant that continuously monitors your workspace and alerts you to important situations that need your attention—or celebrates successes worth recognizing.

Insights help you stay on top of your team's performance without constantly checking every detail manually. They surface critical information at the right time, so you can focus on what matters most: supporting your team and achieving your goals.

---

## Where to Find Your Insights

Insights appear in your **Daily Briefing** view, organized by priority. The most critical insights appear at the top, with color-coded indicators showing their urgency:

- **Red (Critical)**: Immediate attention required
- **Orange (High)**: Address within 1-2 days
- **Blue (Medium)**: Monitor and address when appropriate
- **Green (Low)**: Informational or positive updates

You'll also see a notification badge on your Briefing tab when new insights are available.

## Types of Insights

ProCohere monitors six different areas of your workspace and generates specific insights for each:

### 1. **Task Insights**
*What it watches*: All tasks assigned to your team members

**Overdue Tasks**
- **What it means**: A task has passed its due date without being completed
- **When you'll see it**: The day after a task becomes overdue, and periodically until resolved
- **Example**: "Design Review Overdue - Task is 3 days past due date"
- **What to do**: 
  - Check in with the task owner to understand blockers
  - Adjust the due date if priorities have changed
  - Provide resources or support to help complete the task
  - Consider marking the task as completed if it's no longer relevant

**Stale Tasks**
- **What it means**: A task has been open for 14+ days with no due date set
- **When you'll see it**: Weekly review of tasks older than 14 days
- **Example**: "Update Documentation - No progress for 18 days"
- **What to do**:
  - Set a due date to create accountability
  - Check if the task is still relevant
  - Break down large tasks into smaller, manageable pieces
  - Consider archiving if the task is no longer needed

### 2. **Goal Insights**
*What it watches*: Progress toward your team's goals and objectives

**Goals Off Track**
- **What it means**: A goal is at risk of not being achieved by its target date based on current progress
- **When you'll see it**: When a goal's status is "at-risk" or when a goal is due within 14 days with less than 50% progress
- **Example**: "Q1 Revenue Target - Due in 8 days, only 35% complete"
- **What to do**:
  - Review what's blocking progress with your team
  - Reallocate resources to prioritize this goal
  - Adjust the timeline if external factors have changed
  - Break down remaining work into clear action items
  - Consider whether the goal is still realistic and relevant

**Goals On Track**
- **What it means**: A goal is making excellent progress and is likely to be achieved on time
- **When you'll see it**: When a goal's status is "on-track" with consistent progress
- **Example**: "Customer Satisfaction Improvement - 85% complete, ahead of schedule"
- **What to do**:
  - Celebrate the success with your team
  - Recognize individuals who contributed
  - Document what's working well to replicate on other goals
  - Consider sharing the approach with other teams

### 3. **Meeting Insights**
*What it watches*: Your scheduled one-on-ones and team meetings

**Overdue Meetings**
- **What it means**: A scheduled meeting is 7+ days past its scheduled date without being marked complete
- **When you'll see it**: One week after a meeting becomes overdue
- **Example**: "One-on-One with Sarah - Scheduled for Jan 15, not yet held"
- **What to do**:
  - Reschedule the meeting if it was postponed
  - Mark as complete if the meeting already occurred
  - Consider if meeting cadence needs adjustment
  - Prioritize reconnecting if it's a regular check-in

**Upcoming Meetings**
- **What it means**: An important meeting is scheduled within the next 3 days
- **When you'll see it**: 1-3 days before the meeting
- **Example**: "Quarterly Review with Michael - In 2 days"
- **What to do**:
  - Prepare your agenda and talking points
  - Review any relevant documents or metrics
  - Confirm the meeting is still on the calendar
  - Send any pre-reading materials to participants

### 4. **Metric Insights**
*What it watches*: Key performance indicators and team metrics

**Missing Metric Data**
- **What it means**: A metric exists but doesn't have a current value recorded
- **When you'll see it**: Weekly review of metrics without values
- **Example**: "Customer Retention Rate - No data recorded"
- **What to do**:
  - Update the metric with current data
  - Set up automated data collection if possible
  - Schedule regular metric updates
  - Archive the metric if it's no longer being tracked

**Declining Metrics**
- **What it means**: A metric's current value is below its target by 20% or more
- **When you'll see it**: When metric values are updated and fall significantly below target
- **Example**: "Response Time - Currently 24hrs, target 12hrs (50% below)"
- **What to do**:
  - Investigate the root cause of the decline
  - Create action items to address the issue
  - Adjust targets if they're no longer realistic
  - Monitor closely for continued decline
  - Share the concern with your team and brainstorm solutions

### 5. **Personal Date Insights**
*What it watches*: Important personal milestones for your team members

**Birthdays**
- **What it means**: A team member's birthday is within the next 7 days
- **When you'll see it**: Up to one week before the birthday
- **Example**: "Alex's Birthday - In 3 days (January 28)"
- **What to do**:
  - Send a personal birthday message
  - Organize a team celebration if appropriate
  - Consider a small gift or recognition
  - Update their personal date record if needed

**Work Anniversaries**
- **What it means**: A team member's significant work anniversary is within 7 days
- **When you'll see it**: For anniversaries of 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 years
- **Example**: "Sarah's 5-Year Anniversary - Tomorrow"
- **What to do**:
  - Acknowledge their contribution to the team
  - Consider a public recognition or thank-you
  - Reflect on their growth and achievements
  - Discuss career development opportunities

### 6. **Survey Sentiment Insights**
*What it watches*: Trends in team member survey responses over time

**Declining Sentiment**
- **What it means**: A team member's survey responses show increasingly negative trends over time
- **When you'll see it**: After collecting 2+ survey responses that show deterioration
- **Example**: "James's Engagement Declining - 3 consecutive negative responses"
- **What to do**:
  - Schedule a one-on-one to check in personally
  - Create a safe space for honest conversation
  - Ask open-ended questions about their experience
  - Develop an action plan to address their concerns
  - Follow up regularly to track improvement

**Improving Sentiment**
- **What it means**: A team member's survey responses show consistently positive trends
- **When you'll see it**: After collecting 2+ survey responses that show improvement
- **Example**: "Emma's Satisfaction Improving - Steady positive trend"
- **What to do**:
  - Recognize what's working well
  - Ask what changed to understand the improvement
  - Share successful approaches with other team members
  - Continue the behaviors that led to improvement

## How Insights Are Generated

### Automatic Analysis
Insights are generated automatically by analyzing your workspace data:

- **Task Analysis**: Runs when tasks are created, updated, or become overdue
- **Goal Analysis**: Runs when goals are updated or approach their due dates
- **Meeting Analysis**: Runs daily to check for overdue or upcoming meetings
- **Metric Analysis**: Runs when metric values are updated
- **Personal Date Analysis**: Runs daily to check for upcoming birthdays and anniversaries
- **Survey Analysis**: Runs after new survey responses are submitted

### Intelligence & Relevance
Each insight includes a **relevance score** (0-100%) that indicates how important the insight is:

- **90-100% (Critical)**: Requires immediate action to prevent failure or recognize exceptional performance
- **70-89% (High)**: Should be addressed within 1-2 days
- **40-69% (Medium)**: Monitor and address when appropriate
- **0-39% (Low)**: Informational or minor issues

The system uses intelligent algorithms to calculate relevance based on:
- How overdue or urgent something is
- The impact on goals and team performance
- Historical patterns and trends
- The importance of the related entity (critical goals get higher scores)

### Deduplication
ProCohere automatically prevents duplicate insights. If the same situation is detected multiple times (e.g., the same task is still overdue tomorrow), the insight won't be created again. This keeps your briefing focused on unique, actionable items.

## Working with Insights

### Viewing Insight Details
Click on any insight card to view:
- Full description and context
- Related entity (task, goal, meeting, etc.)
- When the insight was generated
- Recommended actions

### Taking Action on Insights

**Dismiss**
- **What it does**: Removes the insight from your active list permanently
- **When to use**: 
  - You've addressed the issue
  - The insight is no longer relevant
  - You've decided not to take action
- **Result**: The insight moves to your dismissed list and won't reappear

**Snooze**
- **What it does**: Temporarily hides the insight and shows it again tomorrow
- **When to use**:
  - You need more time to address the issue
  - You're waiting on someone else
  - You want a reminder to follow up
- **Result**: The insight disappears and reappears in your next daily briefing
- **Note**: Currently stored as a dismissal; future versions will support custom snooze durations

**View Details**
- **What it does**: Navigates directly to the related entity (task, goal, meeting, etc.)
- **When to use**:
  - You want to see full context
  - You need to update or edit the related item
  - You want to review progress or history
- **Result**: Opens the relevant detail view where you can take action

### Insight Navigation
From any insight, you can jump directly to:
- The task list (for task insights)
- Goal details (for goal insights)
- Meeting details (for meeting insights)
- Metric tracking (for metric insights)
- Team member profiles (for personal date insights)
- Survey results (for sentiment insights)

## Best Practices

### Daily Routine
1. **Start your day with your Briefing** - Check for new critical insights
2. **Prioritize red and orange items** - Address urgent issues first
3. **Dismiss or snooze** - Keep your briefing focused on active items
4. **Take immediate action** - Use "View Details" to jump to the source and resolve issues
5. **Recognize success** - Don't ignore positive insights—celebrate wins

### Weekly Review
- Review all insights from the past week
- Look for patterns (e.g., same person's tasks always overdue)
- Adjust your processes based on recurring insights
- Update goals, metrics, or meeting schedules as needed

### Team Communication
- Share relevant insights with your team
- Use insights as conversation starters in one-on-ones
- Discuss patterns and trends in team meetings
- Create action items based on insight recommendations

### Insight Management
- Keep your briefing clean by dismissing resolved items
- Don't let insights pile up—take action or make decisions
- Use snooze strategically for items that need follow-up
- Check dismissed insights periodically to track what you've addressed

## Understanding Insight Priority

Insights are automatically prioritized based on multiple factors:

### Urgency
- How overdue is something?
- How close is a deadline?
- How quickly is a metric declining?

### Impact
- Is this related to a critical goal?
- Does this affect multiple team members?
- Could this cascade into bigger problems?

### Recency
- When was the insight generated?
- Is this a new situation or ongoing?
- Has the situation gotten worse?

### Type
- Some insight types are inherently more urgent (overdue tasks vs. upcoming birthdays)
- Critical severity always rises to the top

## Privacy & Data

### What Data Is Analyzed
Insights are based on data you and your team have already entered into ProCohere:
- Tasks and their completion status
- Goals and progress updates
- Meeting schedules and attendance
- Metric values and targets
- Personal dates (birthdays, start dates)
- Survey responses

### Who Sees What
- **You see insights generated for you** based on your role and responsibilities
- **Team members only see their own insights** (if they have insight access)
- **Insights respect existing permissions** - you won't see insights about data you can't access
- **Organization isolation** - insights never cross organization boundaries (enforced at database level via RLS)

### Data Security
- All insights are stored securely in your organization's database
- Insights respect the same Row-Level Security (RLS) as all other data
- Dismissed insights are soft-deleted (not permanently removed) for audit purposes
- You can request full deletion of insights through your organization administrator

## Troubleshooting

### "I'm not seeing any insights"
**Possible reasons:**
- Your workspace is new and doesn't have enough data yet
- All your tasks, goals, and meetings are on track (good news!)
- Insights may have been dismissed already
- The insight analyzers haven't run yet (they run periodically throughout the day)

**What to do:**
- Add tasks, goals, meetings, and metrics to your workspace
- Wait for the daily analysis cycle to run
- Check your dismissed insights to see if you previously dismissed them
- Ensure your team members are updating their data regularly

### "I'm seeing duplicate insights"
**Possible reasons:**
- The same issue exists for multiple entities (e.g., multiple overdue tasks)
- Different analyzers are detecting related issues

**What to do:**
- Review each insight—they may be for different items
- Dismiss insights that are no longer relevant
- Address the underlying issue to prevent future duplicates

### "An insight is wrong or outdated"
**Possible reasons:**
- Data was updated after the insight was generated
- The insight was based on incomplete information
- A bug in the analyzer logic

**What to do:**
- Dismiss the insight if it's no longer accurate
- Update the related entity (task, goal, etc.) with current information
- Report the issue to your administrator if it seems like a system error

### "I dismissed an insight by mistake"
**What to do:**
- Contact your organization administrator to restore the insight
- Dismissed insights are never permanently deleted, only hidden
