# Pro Cohere – Digest & Summary System Specification (v1)

This document defines the Digest & Summary System for Pro Cohere.
Digests are designed as a reflective, non-intrusive layer that helps users stay oriented, prepared, and supported—without judgment or evaluation.

## Purpose

The Digest system provides periodic, opt-in summaries of relevant activity, surfaced at predictable intervals.
It is not a notification system, not an alert engine, and not an AI judgment mechanism.

## Design Principles

• Inform, never evaluate  
• Prepare, never overwhelm  
• Surface patterns, not scores  
• Passive by default, user-controlled  
• Role-aware but never comparative at an individual level

## Digest Types (v1)

1. Daily Brief (optional)
2. Weekly Digest (default)
3. Standing Snapshot (on-demand or scheduled)

## Content Sources

Digests may include curated data from:
• Meetings
• Agenda items
• Tasks
• Goals and targets
• Metrics
• Feedback (non-attributable, non-anonymous-safe)
• Upcoming reminders

## Role-Based Framing

ICs:
• Personal goals, tasks, meetings, metrics
• No peer comparison
• No ranking or scoring

Managers:
• Team-level trends only
• Aggregate statistics allowed
• No individual comparisons
• No performance judgments

## AI Involvement

AI may assist with:
• Summarization
• Trend highlighting
• Language softening
• Contextual framing

AI must never:
• Grade performance
• Assign blame
• Predict outcomes
• Recommend discipline

## Tone & Language Guardrails

All digest language must:
• Be descriptive, not prescriptive
• Avoid value judgments
• Use tentative phrasing
• Invite reflection, not action pressure

## Delivery Channels

Primary: In-app view
Secondary: Email (optional)
No push or toast delivery

## Configuration & Settings

• Enable/disable per digest type
• Frequency control
• AI summarization toggle
• Retention window (default 90 days)

## Explicit Exclusions (v1)

• Individual ranking
• Peer comparison
• Sentiment scoring
• Hidden AI triggers
• Automated escalation

## Future Considerations (v2)

• Cross-period comparisons
• Custom digest templates
• Manager-defined focus areas
• Export automation
