# Oracle - Your AI Assistant

**Oracle** is your intelligent AI assistant powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Pro. Oracle doesn't just answer questions—it can take actions, analyze your data with semantic search, and help you manage your team more effectively.

|------|:------:|:-------------:|:-------:|
| Free | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Standard | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Pro | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

## Opening Oracle

### From Any Screen
- Click the **✨** sparkle button (bottom-right corner)
- Or press **F1**

### From the Menu
- Click your profile icon → **Help**

## What Can Oracle Do?

### 🎯 Take Actions (Pro Only)

Oracle can execute tasks for you using natural language:

**Schedule Meetings:**
> "Schedule a 1:1 with Sarah next Monday at 2pm"

> "Set up a meeting with John tomorrow afternoon"

**Create Tasks:**
> "Create a task for Casey to review the budget by Friday"

> "Add a task to update the quarterly report"

**Track Goals:**
> "Create a KPI for customer satisfaction with a target of 90%"

> "Add an OKR for improving team productivity this quarter"

**Search & Query:**
> "Show me my meetings this week"

> "Who's on my team?"

> "Find tasks assigned to John"

Oracle executes these actions immediately and confirms what was created.

### 💬 Help with Features (Standard+)

Ask about how to use Tracker:

> "How do I create an OKR?"

> "What's the difference between tasks and projects?"

> "How do I export my data?"

> "Where can I see my team's KPIs?"

> "How do I set up calendar sync?"

Oracle knows all Tracker features and can guide you step-by-step.

### 📊 Analyze Your Data with Semantic Search (Pro Only)

Oracle uses advanced semantic search to find insights in your data:

> "When did Sarah start working here?"

> "What tasks are assigned to John?"

> "Show me recent feedback for Casey"

> "What OKRs are currently active?"

> "Which KPIs are tracking revenue?"

Unlike simple keyword search, Oracle understands the *meaning* of your questions and finds relevant information even if you don't use exact terms.

### 🎓 Best Practices (Standard+)

Get management advice:

> "How often should I have 1:1s?"

> "What makes a good OKR?"

> "How should I structure feedback?"

> "Tips for effective 1:1 agendas"

**Note**: Actions and data analysis require the Pro plan.

## Using Oracle

### Asking Questions & Giving Commands

1. Type your question or command in the input box
2. Press **Enter** or click **Send**
3. Wait for the response (usually 2-5 seconds)
4. For actions, Oracle will execute and confirm

### Tips for Better Results

**Be specific:**
- ❌ "How do tasks work?"
- ✅ "How do I assign a task to a team member?"

**Use natural language for actions:**
- ✅ "Schedule a 1:1 with Sarah next Tuesday"
- ✅ "Create a task for John to review the budget"
- ✅ "Add a KPI tracking customer retention"

**Provide context:**
- ❌ "Why isn't it working?"
- ✅ "Why isn't my OKR showing progress from linked KPIs?"

**Ask follow-ups:**
- "Can you explain that differently?"
- "Give me an example"
- "What's the keyboard shortcut for that?"

### Conversation History

- Oracle remembers your recent messages
- Ask follow-up questions naturally
- Click **Clear** to start fresh

## Privacy & Security

### What Oracle Can See
- Tracker documentation and features (all plans)
- Your current data (team, tasks, meetings, etc.) - **Pro only**
- Current date and context

### What Oracle Cannot See
- Your password or authentication details
- Other users' data
- Deleted items
- External systems

### How Oracle Works
- **Powered by**: Google Gemini 2.5 Pro
- **Semantic Search**: Your data is vectorized locally for intelligent search
- **Privacy**: Only minimal context is sent to Google's API
- **Security**: No personal credentials are ever transmitted
- **Data Storage**: Embeddings stored locally, not on Google's servers

### Data Handling
- Queries are processed by Google Gemini
- Minimal context is sent (only what's needed to answer)
- No personal data is stored by the AI provider
- Vector embeddings stored locally in your database
- See our [Privacy Policy](https://pricklycactus.com/privacy)

## Troubleshooting

### "AI is not available"
- Check your subscription (requires Standard+)
- Verify internet connection
- Check Settings → AI to ensure it's enabled
- AI may be temporarily disabled

### Slow Responses
- Normal response time: 2-5 seconds
- Actions may take slightly longer (3-7 seconds)
- Check your internet connection
- Complex data queries may take longer

### "I don't have access to your data" or "Actions require Pro"
- Data analysis and actions require Pro plan
- Upgrade to Pro for full Oracle capabilities
- [Compare plans](../account/subscriptions.md)

### Incorrect Information
- AI can occasionally make mistakes
- Verify important information before acting
- Actions can be undone manually if needed
- Report issues via **Report a Problem**

### "Could not find team member"
- Check spelling of names
- Try using first name only
- Use "Show me my team" to see exact names
- Names are case-insensitive but must match

### Pro Plan ($12/user/month)

**Budget Tracking:**
- Oracle shows your monthly usage
- Warnings at 80% of budget
- AI disables when budget exceeded (if enabled in settings)
- Resets monthly
