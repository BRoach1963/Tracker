# Signing In to Tracker

Welcome to Tracker! This guide covers everything you need to know about signing in to your account.

## Sign In Screen

When you launch Tracker, you'll see the sign-in screen with two options:
- **Sign In** - For existing accounts
- **Create Account** - For new users

## Signing In

### With Email and Password

1. Enter your **email address**
2. Enter your **password**
3. Optionally check **Remember me** to stay signed in
4. Click **Sign In**

### Remember Me

When enabled:
- Your email is saved locally
- Your password is securely encrypted on your device
- Next time you launch Tracker, you'll be signed in automatically
- Only works on THIS device and THIS Windows account

When disabled:
- You must enter credentials each time
- More secure for shared computers

### Forgot Password?

If you've forgotten your password:
1. Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Return to Tracker and sign in with your new password

**Note**: Password reset emails are sent immediately. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

## Troubleshooting Sign In

### "Invalid email or password"
- Double-check your email address spelling
- Ensure Caps Lock is off
- Try resetting your password

### "Account not found"
- Verify you're using the correct email
- You may need to create a new account
- Check if you signed up with a different email

### "Too many attempts"
- Wait 15 minutes before trying again
- This protects your account from unauthorized access

### "Network error"
- Check your internet connection
- Tracker requires internet to sign in
- Try again in a few moments

## Security Best Practices

### Strong Passwords
Create a password that:
- Is at least 8 characters long
- Contains uppercase and lowercase letters
- Includes numbers and special characters
- Is unique (not used on other sites)

### When to Disable "Remember Me"
- On shared or public computers
- If others have access to your Windows account
- When using someone else's device

### Signing Out
To sign out:
1. Click your **profile icon** in the top-right
2. Select **Sign Out**
3. Confirm when prompted

This clears your session and any saved credentials.

## Getting Help

If you're having trouble signing in:
- Click the **ⓘ** (info) icon on the login screen
- Email support@pricklycactus.com
- Visit help.pricklycactus.com
