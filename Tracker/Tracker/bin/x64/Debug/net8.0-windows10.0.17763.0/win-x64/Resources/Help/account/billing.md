# Billing & Payments

This guide covers everything about billing, payments, invoices, and managing your Tracker subscription.

## Payment Methods

### Accepted Payment Methods
- **Credit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Debit Cards**: With Visa or Mastercard logo
- **PayPal**: Link your PayPal account
- **Invoice**: Available for Enterprise plans only

### Adding a Payment Method
1. Click your **profile icon**
2. Select **Account**
3. Go to **Billing**
4. Click **Add Payment Method**
5. Enter your card or PayPal details
6. Click **Save**

### Updating Payment Method
1. Go to **Account** → **Billing**
2. Click **Edit** next to your payment method
3. Update the details
4. Click **Save**

### Removing Payment Method
You can remove a payment method if:
- You have another payment method on file, OR
- You're on the Free plan

To remove:
1. Go to **Account** → **Billing**
2. Click **Remove** next to the payment method
3. Confirm removal

## Billing Cycles

### Monthly Billing
- Charged on the same day each month
- Based on your signup date
- Example: Signed up January 15 → Billed on the 15th each month

### Annual Billing
- Charged once per year
- 20% discount vs. monthly
- Example: $67.20/year vs. $84/year for Standard

### Changing Billing Cycle
- Switch from monthly to annual anytime
- Credit applied for unused portion of current month
- Switch from annual to monthly at renewal

## Invoices & Receipts

### Viewing Invoices
1. Go to **Account** → **Billing**
2. Click **Billing History**
3. See all past invoices

### Invoice Details Include
- Invoice number and date
- Plan name and period
- Amount charged
- Payment method used
- Tax information (if applicable)

### Downloading Invoices
1. Go to **Billing History**
2. Click the invoice you need
3. Click **Download PDF**

### Email Receipts
Receipts are automatically emailed to your account email after each payment. To change the billing email:
1. Go to **Account** → **Billing**
2. Update **Billing Email**
3. Click **Save**

## Plan Changes

### Upgrading
When you upgrade:
- New features available immediately
- Prorated charge for remainder of billing period
- Example: Upgrade mid-month, pay half the price difference

### Downgrading
When you downgrade:
- Takes effect at end of current billing period
- Keep higher-tier features until then
- No refund for unused time

### Canceling
When you cancel:
- Access continues until end of billing period
- Data preserved (you can reactivate later)
- After 90 days of inactivity, data may be deleted

## Refunds

### Refund Policy
- **14-day money-back guarantee** on first payment
- Prorated refunds for annual plans cancelled within 30 days
- No refunds for monthly plans (you keep access until period ends)

### Requesting a Refund
1. Email billing@pricklycactus.com
2. Include your account email
3. Reason for refund (optional but helpful)
4. Processed within 5-10 business days

## Taxes

### Sales Tax
- Charged based on your location
- Required by law in certain regions
- Displayed before you complete purchase

### Tax Exempt Status
If your organization is tax-exempt:
1. Email billing@pricklycactus.com
2. Attach tax-exempt certificate
3. We'll update your account within 48 hours

### VAT (European Union)
- VAT charged for EU customers
- Provide VAT ID to remove VAT (business customers)
- Go to **Account** → **Billing** → **Tax Information**

## Troubleshooting

### Payment Failed
Common reasons:
- **Insufficient funds**: Add funds or use different card
- **Card expired**: Update payment method
- **Card declined**: Contact your bank
- **Incorrect details**: Verify card number, CVV, expiration

After fixing:
1. Go to **Account** → **Billing**
2. Click **Retry Payment**

### Duplicate Charges
If you see duplicate charges:
1. Check if you have multiple accounts
2. Review billing history for details
3. Contact billing@pricklycactus.com with:
   - Your account email
   - Date of charges
   - Amount

We'll investigate and refund any errors.

### Missing Invoice
If an invoice isn't in your billing history:
1. Check your email (including spam)
2. Verify you're logged into correct account
3. Contact billing@pricklycactus.com

### Subscription Not Active
If features aren't working after payment:
1. Sign out and sign back in
2. Check **Account** for subscription status
3. Contact support if issue persists

## Enterprise Billing

### Invoice Terms
Enterprise customers can pay by invoice:
- Net 30 payment terms
- Annual billing only
- Minimum 50 seats
- Contact enterprise@pricklycactus.com

### Purchase Orders
We accept POs for enterprise purchases:
1. Email PO to billing@pricklycactus.com
2. Include company details and billing contact
3. Invoice sent within 2 business days
