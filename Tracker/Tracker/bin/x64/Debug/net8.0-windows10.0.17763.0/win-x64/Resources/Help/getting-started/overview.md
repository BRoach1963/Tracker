# Tracker Overview

Tracker is a comprehensive team management application designed for managers to effectively track, develop, and support their direct reports. Built with a focus on manager workflows, Tracker centralizes everything you need to be an effective people leader.

## Why Tracker?

Managing people is challenging. You need to:
- Remember conversations from months ago
- Track goals and commitments
- Prepare for meaningful 1:1s
- Give timely, specific feedback
- Monitor team performance
- Balance competing priorities

Tracker solves this by providing a single source of truth for your team data.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create Your Account
- Sign up with email and password
- Verify your email
- Choose your subscription plan

### 2. Complete Setup
- Run through the Setup Wizard
- Choose where to store your data
- Optionally add sample data to explore

### 3. Start Managing
- Add your team members
- Schedule your first 1:1

## Core Features

### 👥 Team Management
Your team members are at the center of everything:
- **Rich profiles**: Name, role, skills, contact info, photo
- **Complete history**: All interactions, meetings, feedback in one place
- **Relationship tracking**: Manager relationships and reporting structure
- **Skill inventory**: Track competencies and expertise areas

### 📅 1:1 Meetings
The foundation of great management:
- **Schedule recurring 1:1s** with each team member
- **Build agendas** before meetings
- **Take rich notes** during conversations
- **Link to context**: Connect agenda items to tasks, OKRs, projects
- **Track follow-ups**: Never lose commitments made in meetings

### ✅ Tasks
Track individual work items:
- **Standalone or project-based** tasks
- **Priority levels**: Critical, High, Medium, Low
- **Status workflow**: Not Started → In Progress → Complete
- **Due date tracking** with overdue alerts
- **Assignment** to team members

### 📊 Projects
Manage larger initiatives:
- **Multi-task containers** with overall progress
- **Team assignment** and role tracking
- **Timeline management** with start/end dates
- **Status tracking**: Not Started, In Progress, On Hold, Completed
- **Link to OKRs** for strategic alignment

### 🎯 OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
Align work to goals:
- **Objectives**: Qualitative goals ("Improve customer satisfaction")
- **Key Results**: Measurable outcomes ("Increase NPS from 7 to 8.5")
- **Automatic progress** from linked data sources
- **Status indicators**: On Track, At Risk, Off Track
- **Quarterly/annual cycles** with history

### 📈 KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)
Track what matters:
- **Define metrics** with targets
- **Visual status**: On Target (🟢), Close (🟡), Off Target (🔴)
- **Composite KPIs** calculated from child metrics
- **Frequency tracking**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, On-Demand
- **Link to Key Results** for OKR automation

### 🎓 Goals & Feedback
Develop your people:
- **Individual goals**: Career development, skill building
- **SMART goal tracking** with milestones
- **Feedback records**: Recognition, coaching, performance
- **SBI framework** support (Situation-Behavior-Impact)
- **Performance review prep**: All data in one place

### 📝 Quick Notes
Capture everything else:
- **Fast capture**: `Ctrl+Shift+N` from anywhere
- **Rich formatting**: Bold, lists, checkboxes
- **Tags and colors** for organization
- **Convert to**: Tasks, agenda items, feedback

### 📊 Reports (Standard+)
Analyze and share:
- **Built-in reports**: Team summary, OKR progress, KPI scorecards
- **Custom reports**: Build exactly what you need (Pro)
- **Export options**: PDF, Excel, PowerPoint (Pro)
- **Scheduled delivery**: Automated email reports (Pro)

### 🤖 Oracle - AI Assistant (Standard+)
Get instant assistance:
- **Feature guidance**: Learn how to use any feature
- **Best practices**: Get management tips
- **Data analysis**: Ask about your team data (Pro only)
- Press **F1** anytime to open

## Navigation

Tracker is organized into **five pillars**:

| Pillar | Icon | Purpose | Shortcut |
|--------|------|---------|----------|
| **Home** | 🏠 | Dashboard and overview | `Alt+1` |
| **Circle** | 👥 | Team, 1:1s, Feedback, Goals | `Alt+2` |
| **Pulse** | 📊 | Projects, Tasks, OKRs, KPIs | `Alt+3` |
| **Chronicle** | 📝 | Notes and Reports | `Alt+4` |
| **Settings** | ⚙️ | Configuration | `Alt+5` |

## Quick Start

1. **Create your account** (or sign in)
2. **Complete the Setup Wizard**
3. **Add your team members** (Circle → Team)
4. **Schedule your first 1:1** (Circle → 1:1s)
5. **Create some tasks** (Pulse → Tasks)
6. **Set up OKRs** (Pulse → OKRs)
7. **Check your Dashboard** (Home)

## Getting Help

- Press **F1** anytime for the AI Help Assistant (Standard+)
- The assistant knows your app features AND your data (Pro)
- Ask questions like "Who has a 1:1 this week?" or "How do I set up OKRs?"
- Click the **ⓘ** (info) icon on any screen for context-sensitive help

## Data Privacy

Your data, your control:
- **Local storage by default** - Data on your machine
- **You control** backups and exports
- **No cloud sync** required (optional with Pro)
- **AI assistance** sends only necessary context
- **Delete anytime** - Your data is yours

## System Requirements

- **OS**: Windows 10 (version 1903+) or Windows 11
- **Memory**: 4 GB RAM minimum
- **Storage**: 100 MB + your data
- **Display**: 1280x720 minimum
- **Internet**: Required for sign-in, AI features, and sync
