# Quick Start Guide

Get up and running with Tracker in 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 min)

1. Launch Tracker
2. Click **Create Account**
3. Enter your:
   - Display name
   - Email address
   - Password
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Check your email and verify your account

**Tip**: Use your work email for business use.

## Step 2: Complete Setup (1 min)

The Setup Wizard guides you through initial configuration:

1. **Choose Database Location**
   - Local (default) - Data stays on your computer
   - Custom location - Choose a specific folder
   
2. **Sample Data (Optional)**
   - Check "Include sample data" to see how Tracker works
   - Uncheck for a clean start

3. Click **Finish**

## Step 3: Add Your First Team Member (1 min)

1. Go to **Circle** → **Team**
2. Click **+ Add Team Member**
3. Enter basic info:
   - Name (required)
   - Role/Title
   - Email
4. Click **Save**

Repeat for each team member.

## Step 4: Schedule a 1:1 (1 min)

1. Go to **Circle** → **1:1s**
2. Click **+ New 1:1**
3. Select the team member
4. Set date and time
5. Add agenda items (optional)
6. Click **Save**

## You're Ready!

That's it! You now have:
- ✅ A Tracker account
- ✅ Your database set up
- ✅ A team member added
- ✅ Your first 1:1 scheduled
