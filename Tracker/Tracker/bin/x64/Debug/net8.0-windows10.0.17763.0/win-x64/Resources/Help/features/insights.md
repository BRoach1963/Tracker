# Proactive AI Insights

**Proactive AI Insights** is your intelligent early-warning system that monitors your team management data and surfaces important items requiring attention—before you miss them.

## Overview

Instead of manually reviewing every team member, meeting, and task, Tracker's AI continuously analyzes your data and proactively alerts you to:

- **Missed 1:1s** - Team members you haven't met with recently
- **Upcoming birthdays** - Celebrate your team's special days
- **Work anniversaries** - Recognize tenure milestones
- **Stale action items** - Tasks that need follow-up
- **Overdue deliverables** - Items past their due date

## The Daily Briefing

### What Is It?

The **Daily Briefing** is a personalized summary that appears when you open Tracker each day. Think of it as your morning "manager checklist" that highlights what needs attention today.

### What's Included

The Daily Briefing shows:

1. **Quick Stats**
   - Number of meetings scheduled today
   - Count of open action items
   - Total active insights

2. **Needs Attention** (Critical items)
   - Urgent items that may affect team relationships or deliverables
   - Displayed with a red indicator

3. **Reminders** (Warning items)
   - Important items approaching deadlines or thresholds
   - Displayed with an orange indicator

4. **Good to Know** (Informational)
   - Helpful awareness items like upcoming birthdays
   - Displayed with a blue indicator

### Dismissing the Daily Briefing

- Click **"Let's Go!"** to close and start your day
- Check **"Don't show on startup"** if you prefer to check insights manually
- You can re-enable it anytime in Settings

## The Insight Badge

### Where to Find It

A small red badge appears on the **✨ sparkle button** (bottom-right corner) when you have unread insights. The number shows how many insights are waiting for your attention.

### Badge Colors

- **Red with number** - You have unread insights
- **No badge** - All caught up!

## Understanding Insight Types

### 🗓️ Meeting Gap Insights

**What it detects:** Team members you haven't had a 1:1 meeting with recently.

**Why it matters:** Regular 1:1s are crucial for team engagement, coaching, and catching issues early. Missing them can lead to disconnected team members.

**Severity levels:**
| Days Since Last 1:1 | Severity | Indicator |
|---------------------|----------|-----------|
| 14+ days | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |
| 21+ days | 🔴 Critical | Red |

**Example insight:**
> **⚠️ Check in with Sarah**
> It's been 18 days since your last 1:1 with Sarah Johnson. Consider scheduling a meeting soon.

**Suggested action:** Click the insight to navigate to the team member, then schedule a 1:1.

---

### 🎂 Birthday Insights

**What it detects:** Team members with birthdays in the next 7 days.

**Why it matters:** Acknowledging birthdays builds team morale and shows you care about your people as individuals.

**Severity:** Always informational (blue indicator)

**Example insight:**
> **🎂 Casey's birthday is coming up!**
> Casey Martinez turns 32 on January 3rd. Don't forget to wish them a happy birthday!

**Suggested action:** Set a personal reminder, plan a celebration, or prepare a card/gift.

---

### 🎉 Work Anniversary Insights

**What it detects:** Team members approaching work anniversaries, with special emphasis on milestone years.

**Why it matters:** Recognizing tenure shows appreciation and reinforces commitment.

**Milestone years highlighted:**
- 1 year (first anniversary)
- 5 years
- 10 years
- 15, 20, 25+ years

**Severity levels:**
| Type | Severity | Indicator |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Regular anniversary | ℹ️ Info | Blue |
| Milestone (1, 5, 10+ years) | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |

**Example insight:**
> **🎉 John's 5-year anniversary!**
> John Smith celebrates 5 years with the company on January 15th. This milestone deserves special recognition!

**Suggested action:** Plan recognition appropriate to the milestone—announcement, gift, or celebration.

---

### ✅ Stale Action Item Insights

**What it detects:** Meeting tasks/action items that haven't been completed and are getting old.

**Why it matters:** Uncommitted action items from 1:1s erode trust and accountability. Following up shows you care about agreed-upon commitments.

**Severity levels:**
| Task Age | Severity | Indicator |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| 7+ days overdue | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |
| 14+ days overdue | 🔴 Critical | Red |

**Example insight:**
> **📋 Stale action item**
> "Update quarterly report" from your meeting with Sarah on Dec 10th is now 12 days overdue.

**Suggested action:** Follow up with the assignee or reassess if the task is still relevant.

---

### 🎯 OKR At-Risk Insights

**What it detects:** OKRs that are projected to miss their targets based on current progress velocity.

**Why it matters:** OKRs represent your most important objectives. Early warning of trajectory problems lets you course-correct before it's too late.

**How it works:**
The system calculates your daily progress rate and projects where you'll end up at the OKR deadline. If the projection falls short, you get an alert.

**Severity levels:**
| Projected Final | Severity | Indicator |
|-----------------|----------|-----------|
| 50-69% of target | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |
| Below 50% of target | 🔴 Critical | Red |

**Example insight:**
> **🎯 OKR at risk: Improve Customer Sat...**
> "Improve Customer Satisfaction" is significantly behind. Current progress is 25% and at this pace, projected to reach only 45% by the end date (Mar 31).

**Suggested action:** Review Key Results, identify blockers, consider adjusting targets or reallocating resources.

---

### ⏱️ OKR Ending Soon Insights

**What it detects:** OKR periods that are approaching their end date.

**Why it matters:** As an OKR period ends, you need to either make a final push or prepare for retrospective and next-period planning.

**Severity levels:**
| Days Remaining | Severity | Indicator |
|----------------|----------|-----------|
| 8-14 days | ℹ️ Info | Blue |
| 7 days or less | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |

**Example insight:**
> **⏱️ Soon: OKR ends in 5 days**
> "Improve Customer Satisfaction" ends on Jan 15, 2025. Current progress is 72% (on track). Final sprint to close out remaining items.

**Suggested action:** Focus on achievable Key Results or document learnings for future OKRs.

---

### 📊 KPI Off-Target Insights

**What it detects:** KPIs that are significantly below their target values.

**Why it matters:** KPIs are leading indicators of business health. When a KPI drifts far from target, it often signals a problem requiring intervention.

**Severity levels:**
| Progress vs Target | Severity | Indicator |
|--------------------|----------|-----------|
| 50-69% of target | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |
| Below 50% of target | 🔴 Critical | Red |

**Example insight:**
> **📊 KPI significantly off target: Customer NPS**
> "Customer NPS" is currently at 42.0 points, which is below the target of 65.0 points (65% of target). Gap: 23.0 points.

**Suggested action:** Review the KPI trend and identify root causes. Consider action plans or adjust the target if circumstances have changed.

---

### 📝 Survey Alert Insights

**What it detects:** Low ratings in pulse survey responses that may indicate team member concerns.

**Why it matters:** Survey responses are direct feedback from your team. Low ratings are signals that something may need attention—don't let them go unaddressed.

**Severity levels:**
| Low Response Count | Severity | Indicator |
|--------------------|----------|-----------|
| 1 response | ℹ️ Info | Blue |
| 2 responses | ⚠️ Warning | Orange |
| 3+ responses | 🔴 Critical | Red |

**What counts as "low":**
- Rating questions: 2 or below (on a 5-point scale)
- NPS questions: 6 or below (detractor range)

**Example insight:**
> **📝 Low survey ratings (Engagement)**
> The question "I feel supported in my professional development" received low ratings (avg: 1.5) from 2 anonymous response(s) in the "Q4 Pulse Check" survey.

**Suggested action:** 
- For anonymous surveys: Address concerns in a team meeting without singling anyone out
- For non-anonymous surveys: Follow up individually to understand and help

**Privacy note:** Anonymous survey responses never reveal the respondent's identity, even in insights.

---

### ⏰ Overdue Task Insights

**What it detects:** Tasks with due dates that have passed.

**Why it matters:** Overdue items can cascade into bigger problems. Early awareness helps you intervene.

**Severity:** Critical (red indicator) for any overdue task

**Example insight:**
> **⏰ Overdue: Budget Review**
> This task was due on December 20th (8 days ago). It may be blocking other work.

**Suggested action:** Check on status, offer help removing blockers, or adjust the deadline if circumstances changed.

## Managing Insights

### Viewing All Insights

1. Click the **✨ sparkle button** with the badge
2. Oracle opens showing your insights
3. Or ask: "Show me my insights"

### Marking as Read

- Insights are automatically marked as read when you view them
- Click **"Mark All Read"** to clear the unread count

### Dismissing Insights

Click the **✕** button on any insight to dismiss it. Dismissed insights:
- Won't appear in your list anymore
- Won't be regenerated (until conditions change significantly)
- Can't be undone

**When to dismiss:**
- You've already handled the situation
- The insight is no longer relevant
- You're aware and taking action separately

### Acting on Insights

Click an insight to navigate directly to the related item:
- Team member insights → Opens that person's profile
- Meeting insights → Opens the meeting details
- Task insights → Opens the task

## Customizing Insights

### Opening Settings

1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Find **Insights** section

### Available Settings

| Setting | Description | Default |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Enable Insights** | Turn the entire feature on/off | On |
| **Show Daily Briefing** | Show summary on startup | On |
| **Meeting Gap Warning** | Days before warning appears | 14 days |
| **Meeting Gap Critical** | Days before critical alert | 21 days |
| **Birthday Look-ahead** | Days before birthday to notify | 7 days |
| **Action Item Stale** | Days before task is "stale" | 7 days |

### Adjusting Thresholds

**For frequent 1:1 schedules:**
If you meet weekly, you might want tighter thresholds:
- Meeting Gap Warning: 10 days
- Meeting Gap Critical: 14 days

**For less frequent schedules:**
If you meet bi-weekly or monthly:
- Meeting Gap Warning: 21 days
- Meeting Gap Critical: 30 days

## How Insights Are Generated

### Automatic Analysis

Tracker analyzes your data:
1. **On startup** - Immediately after you log in
2. **Periodically** - Every 4 hours while running
3. **After changes** - When you add/update team members, meetings, or tasks

### Deduplication

The same insight won't appear multiple times. For example, if you already have a "meeting gap" warning for Sarah, you won't get another one until:
- You dismiss it
- The situation changes (you have a meeting)
- It escalates to critical

### Privacy & Data

- All analysis happens **locally** on your computer
- Insights are stored in a local database
- No team data is sent to external servers for insight generation

## Using Oracle to Manage Insights

**Pro Plan Feature**: Oracle, your AI assistant, can help you view and manage your insights using natural language.

### Viewing Insights via Oracle

Open Oracle (click the ✨ button or press **F1**) and ask:

> "What insights do I have?"

> "Show me my critical alerts"

> "Any upcoming birthdays on my team?"

> "Who haven't I met with recently?"

> "What warnings should I know about?"

Oracle will list your active insights grouped by severity, making it easy to see what needs attention first.

### Filtering Insights

You can filter by severity or type:

**By Severity:**
> "Show me my critical insights"

> "What warnings do I have?"

> "Any informational insights?"

**By Type:**
> "Show me meeting gap alerts"

> "Who has upcoming birthdays?"

> "Any stale action items?"

> "Show work anniversary reminders"

### Dismissing Insights via Oracle

Once you've handled an insight, dismiss it through Oracle:

> "Dismiss insight #3"

> "Clear the birthday reminder for Casey"

> "Remove the meeting gap alert"

Oracle will confirm the dismissal and the insight will no longer appear.

### Why Use Oracle for Insights?

- **Hands-free management** - Review insights without clicking through menus
- **Quick triage** - Ask for critical items only when you're short on time
- **Natural workflow** - Handle insights as part of your Oracle conversation
- **Contextual actions** - After seeing an insight, ask Oracle to schedule the meeting or create a task

### Example Workflow

1. Open Oracle: **F1**
2. Ask: *"What critical insights do I have?"*
3. See: Meeting gap with Sarah (18 days)
4. Ask: *"Schedule a 1:1 with Sarah tomorrow at 2pm"*
5. Ask: *"Dismiss the meeting gap insight for Sarah"*
6. Done! Meeting scheduled and insight cleared.

## Tips for Getting the Most from Insights

### 1. Check Daily
Make the Daily Briefing part of your morning routine. It takes 30 seconds and ensures nothing slips through.

### 2. Act on Critical Items First
Red indicators mean something needs attention soon. Address these before they become bigger issues.

### 3. Don't Ignore Warnings
Orange items will become red if not addressed. Better to handle them early.

### 4. Dismiss What You've Handled
Keep your insight list clean by dismissing items you've already addressed. This makes remaining items easier to scan.

### 5. Customize for Your Style
Adjust thresholds to match your management cadence. Too many false alerts? Increase the thresholds. Missing important items? Decrease them.

## Troubleshooting

### "I'm not seeing any insights"

1. **Check if enabled:** Go to Settings → Insights → Enable Insights
2. **Check your data:** Insights require team members with meeting history and personal dates (birthdays, hire dates)
3. **Wait for analysis:** Initial analysis runs on startup; give it a minute

### "I keep getting the same insight"

- Make sure you're dismissing insights you've handled
- If it keeps returning, the underlying condition still exists (e.g., still no meeting scheduled)

### "The daily briefing is annoying"

- Uncheck "Show Daily Briefing on startup" in the dialog
- Or disable it in Settings → Insights → Show Daily Briefing

### "Thresholds don't match my schedule"

Go to Settings and adjust the day thresholds to match your actual meeting frequency.
