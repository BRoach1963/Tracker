# Creating Your Tracker Account

Welcome to Tracker! This guide walks you through creating your account and getting started.

## Why Create an Account?

A Tracker account lets you:
- **Sync your data** across devices (Pro tier)
- **Access your subscription** features
- **Get support** when you need help
- **Receive updates** and important notifications

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Open Create Account

From the sign-in screen:
1. Click the **Create Account** tab
2. You'll see the registration form

### Step 2: Enter Your Information

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Display Name** | How you'll appear in the app (e.g., "John Smith") | Yes |
| **Email** | Your email address - used for sign in and notifications | Yes |
| **Password** | Your secure password | Yes |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter to verify | Yes |

### Step 3: Choose Password

Create a strong password:
- **Minimum 8 characters**
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase
- Include numbers (0-9)
- Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)

**Good example**: `Manager2025!`  
**Bad example**: `password123`

### Step 4: Complete Registration

1. Review your information
2. Click **Create Account**
3. Check your email for verification
4. Click the verification link
5. Return to Tracker and sign in

## After Registration

### Email Verification
- A verification email is sent immediately
- Click the link to verify your account
- The link expires in 24 hours
- Request a new link if it expires

### Your Subscription
- New accounts start on the **Free** tier with a fresh subscription
- Each account has its own independent subscription
- Upgrade anytime from Account Settings
- See [Subscription Plans](subscriptions.md) for details

### First Launch
After signing in for the first time:
1. Complete the **Setup Wizard**
2. Choose your database location
3. Optionally add sample data
4. Start using Tracker!

## Account Security

### Session Management
- Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity
- Sign out remotely from Account Settings
- View active sessions and devices

## Troubleshooting

### "Email already exists"
- An account with this email already exists
- Try signing in instead
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed

### "Password too weak"
- Add more characters
- Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
- Avoid common words

### "Verification email not received"
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Verify email address is correct
3. Click "Resend verification"
4. Wait a few minutes
5. Contact support if still not received

### "Verification link expired"
1. Return to the sign-in screen
2. Click "Resend verification"
3. Use the new link within 24 hours

## Managing Multiple Accounts

### Personal vs. Work
You can create separate accounts for:
- Personal team management
- Different organizations
- Testing environments

### Switching Accounts
1. Sign out from current account
2. Sign in with different credentials
3. Each account has completely isolated data and settings
4. Database preferences are stored per-account

## Data Privacy

When you create an account:
- Your email is used for account management only
- We never sell or share your information
- You can delete your account anytime
- See our [Privacy Policy](https://pricklycactus.com/privacy)
