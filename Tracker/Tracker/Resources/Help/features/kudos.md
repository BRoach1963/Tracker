# Kudos & Recognition

Send recognition and kudos to your team members directly through Teams or Slack.

## Overview

Kudos lets you acknowledge great work by your team members. Unlike internal notes that only you see, kudos can be delivered directly to the team member via:

- **Microsoft Teams** - Direct message to the team member
- **Slack** - Direct message to the team member
- **Internal Only** - Logged in Tracker for your records (not delivered)

## Sending Kudos

### From a Team Member

1. Open a **Team Member** dialog
2. Click the **🌟 Send Kudos** button in the footer
3. The team member will be pre-selected
4. Choose a **Category** for the recognition
5. Write your **Message**
6. Select a **Delivery Channel** (Teams, Slack, or Internal Only)
7. Click **Send Kudos**

### Kudos Categories

| Category | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| 🤝 Team Work | Collaboration and helping others |
| 💡 Innovation | Creative solutions and new ideas |
| 👑 Leadership | Taking initiative and guiding others |
| 🎯 Customer Focus | Going above and beyond for customers |
| 🚀 Going Above & Beyond | Extra effort and exceptional work |
| 🔧 Problem Solving | Tackling difficult challenges |
| 📚 Learning & Growth | Skill development and improvement |
| ⏰ Reliability | Consistent, dependable performance |
| 💬 Communication | Clear and effective communication |

## Delivery Channels

### Microsoft Teams

**Setup Required**: Connect to Microsoft 365

When you're connected to Microsoft 365, kudos are sent as **direct 1:1 messages** in Teams. The team member receives a rich formatted message with:
- A personalized title
- Your kudos message
- The category
- Your name as the sender

**To connect to Microsoft 365**:
1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **Integrations** tab
2. Under "Microsoft 365 (Outlook & Teams)", click **Connect Microsoft 365**
3. Sign in with your Microsoft work or school account
4. Grant the requested permissions

Once connected, Teams kudos work automatically - no additional setup needed!

### Slack

**Setup Required**: Tracker uses a pre-configured Slack bot

When Slack is connected, kudos are sent as **direct messages** in Slack. The team member receives a Block Kit formatted message.

**How it works**:
1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **Integrations** tab
2. Under "Slack", click **Connect Slack**
3. Tracker validates the bot connection
4. You'll see your workspace name when connected

**Important**: The team member's email in Tracker must match their Slack account email for the message to be delivered.

### Internal Only

Choose "Internal Only" to log kudos for your records without sending a notification to the team member. This is useful for:
- Tracking recognition you want to mention later
- Building a history for performance reviews
- Private notes about team member achievements

## Viewing Kudos History

Kudos you've sent are stored in Tracker. You can:

- View kudos history for a team member in their profile
- Include kudos in meeting prep notes
- Reference past recognition during 1:1 meetings

## Troubleshooting

### Teams Kudos

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Teams not available" | Connect to Microsoft 365 in Settings → Integrations tab |
| Message not delivered | Verify team member's email is correct |
| Permission error | You may need admin consent - contact IT |

### Slack Kudos

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Slack not connected" | Connect Slack in Settings → Integrations tab |
| "User not found" | Team member's email must match their Slack email |
| Message failed | Check Slack workspace permissions |

### General

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Delivery status "Failed" | Check connection status, try resending |
| Can't select channel | That integration isn't connected yet |

---

## Privacy & Visibility

- **Kudos you send**: Visible only to you and the recipient
- **Delivery**: Goes directly to team member, not to channels
- **Storage**: Kept in your Tracker database for your records
