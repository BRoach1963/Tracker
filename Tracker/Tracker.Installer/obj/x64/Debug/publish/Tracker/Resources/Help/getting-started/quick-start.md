# Quick Start Guide

Get up and running with Tracker in just a few minutes!

## Step 1: Add Your Team

1. Click **Team Members** in the sidebar
2. Click the **Add Team Member** button
3. Fill in the required information:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Role
   - Hire Date
4. Click **Save**

## Step 2: Schedule Your First 1:1

1. Click **1:1s** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add 1:1**
3. Select a team member
4. Choose a date and time
5. Add agenda items you want to discuss
6. Click **Save**

## Step 3: Create Tasks

1. Click **Tasks** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Task**
3. Enter the task description
4. Assign to a team member
5. Set a due date
6. Click **Save**

## Step 4: Review Your Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick overview of:

- **Upcoming 1:1s** - Meetings scheduled for the next 7 days
- **Overdue Tasks** - Tasks past their due date
- **Team Engagement** - Team members who haven't had a recent 1:1
- **Recent Activity** - Latest updates and changes

## Tips for Success

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Schedule recurring 1:1s to ensure regular check-ins with each team member.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Use the Notes feature to capture quick thoughts and ideas between meetings.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Link tasks and OKRs to 1:1 meetings for comprehensive tracking.
