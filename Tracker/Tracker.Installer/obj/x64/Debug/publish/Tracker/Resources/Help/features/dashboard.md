# Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of your team's status and highlights items requiring your attention.

## Overview

The Dashboard is divided into several sections:

## Upcoming 1:1s

Shows meetings scheduled for the next 7 days:

- **Team Member** - Who you're meeting with
- **Date/Time** - When it's scheduled
- **Quick Actions** - Open or reschedule

## Engagement Alerts

Highlights team members who may need attention:

- **No Recent 1:1** - Haven't met in over 2 weeks
- **Overdue Tasks** - Have tasks past their due date
- **At-Risk Goals** - Goals that may not be met

## Task Summary

Quick view of task status across your team:

- **Open** - Tasks not yet completed
- **Due Soon** - Tasks due in the next 7 days
- **Overdue** - Tasks past their due date
- **Completed This Week** - Recently finished tasks

## Recent Activity

Timeline of recent changes:

- New team members added
- Meetings completed
- Tasks created/completed
- Feedback given

## Statistics Cards

Key metrics at a glance:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Team Size** | Number of active team members |
| **1:1s This Month** | Meetings completed this month |
| **Tasks Completed** | Tasks finished this week |
| **Open Tasks** | Total outstanding tasks |

## Using the Dashboard

### Quick Navigation

Click any item to navigate directly:
- Click a team member name → Team Member profile
- Click a meeting → 1:1 details
- Click a task → Task details

### Refresh Data

The dashboard updates automatically, but you can force a refresh:
- Press **F5**, or
- Click the **Refresh** button

## Best Practices

> 💡 **Check the dashboard daily** - Stay on top of upcoming meetings and overdue items.

> 💡 **Address engagement alerts promptly** - Team members without recent 1:1s may feel disconnected.

> 💡 **Use it as your starting point** - The dashboard guides you to what needs attention.
