# OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)

Set and track Objectives and Key Results for your team.

## Overview

OKRs help align team efforts with organizational goals:

- **Objective** - What you want to achieve (qualitative)
- **Key Results** - How you'll measure success (quantitative)

## Creating an OKR

1. Navigate to **OKRs** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add OKR**
3. Enter the Objective:
   - **Title** - Clear, inspiring goal
   - **Description** - Additional context
   - **Owner** - Team member responsible
   - **Start/End Date** - OKR period
4. Add Key Results:
   - **Description** - Measurable outcome
   - **Target Value** - Success metric
   - **Current Value** - Progress
5. Click **Save**

## Understanding OKRs

### Good Objectives

✅ "Improve customer satisfaction"
✅ "Launch mobile app"
✅ "Reduce technical debt"

### Good Key Results

✅ "Increase NPS from 30 to 50"
✅ "Ship v1.0 by Q2 end"
✅ "Reduce critical bugs by 40%"

## Tracking Progress

### Updating Key Results

1. Expand the OKR row to see Key Results
2. Update current values as progress is made
3. Overall OKR progress auto-calculates

### Status Indicators

- 🟢 **On Track** - Progress ≥ 70%
- 🟡 **At Risk** - Progress 40-69%
- 🔴 **Off Track** - Progress < 40%

## OKR Reviews

Discuss OKRs in 1:1s:

1. Link OKR to meeting
2. Review progress together
3. Adjust targets if needed
4. Create action items

## Best Practices

> 💡 **Limit to 3-5 OKRs** - Focus creates results.

> 💡 **Make Key Results measurable** - Numbers, not adjectives.

> 💡 **Review weekly** - Catch issues early.

> 💡 **Stretch but achievable** - 70% completion is success.

## OKR Cycles

Common OKR periods:
- **Quarterly** - Most common, good cadence
- **Annual** - For strategic objectives
- **Monthly** - For fast-moving teams
