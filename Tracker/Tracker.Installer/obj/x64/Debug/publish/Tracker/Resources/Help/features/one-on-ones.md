# One-on-One Meetings

One-on-one meetings are the cornerstone of effective team management. Tracker helps you schedule, prepare for, and document these important conversations.

## Overview

A 1:1 meeting in Tracker includes:

- **Team Member** - Who you're meeting with
- **Date & Time** - When the meeting is scheduled
- **Agenda Items** - Topics to discuss
- **Tasks** - Action items from the meeting
- **Status** - Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled

## Scheduling a 1:1

1. Navigate to **1:1s** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add 1:1**
3. Select the team member
4. Choose date and time
5. Set the duration (default: 30 minutes)
6. Click **Save**

### Using Templates

Speed up meeting creation with templates:

1. In the Add 1:1 dialog, select a **Template**
2. Pre-defined agenda items will be added
3. Customize as needed

## Preparing for a Meeting

### Adding Agenda Items

1. Click **Add Agenda Item**
2. Choose a category:
   - **Topic** - General discussion point
   - **Question** - Something to ask
   - **Concern** - Issue to address
   - **Update** - Status to share
   - **Feedback** - Positive or constructive
3. Enter the description
4. Set priority (High, Medium, Low)

### Rollover from Previous Meeting

1. Click **Load Previous Meeting**
2. Uncompleted tasks from the last 1:1 will appear
3. Click **Rollover Tasks** to bring them into this meeting

## During the Meeting

### Taking Notes

- Add notes to individual agenda items as you discuss them
- Mark items as resolved when completed
- Create new tasks for follow-up actions

### Creating Tasks

1. Click **Add Task** on the Tasks tab
2. Enter the task description
3. Set the due date
4. Assign the owner (you or the team member)
5. The task is automatically linked to this 1:1

## After the Meeting

1. Review all agenda items are addressed
2. Ensure tasks have clear owners and due dates
3. Change status to **Completed**
4. Notes and tasks are saved for future reference

## Meeting History

View past meetings from:

- **1:1s list** - Shows all meetings
- **Team Member profile** - Shows meetings for that person

## Best Practices

> 💡 **Prepare agenda items before the meeting** - Add topics throughout the week as they come up.

> 💡 **Start with their items first** - Let team members drive the conversation.

> 💡 **Follow up on previous action items** - Use rollover to ensure continuity.

> 💡 **End with clear next steps** - Every meeting should have defined action items.
