# Team Members

The Team Members feature is the foundation of Tracker - everything else connects to your people.

## Overview

Team Members represent your direct reports and key collaborators. Each member profile contains:

- **Basic Info**: Name, email, phone, photo
- **Role Details**: Job title, department, hire date
- **Relationships**: Manager, reporting structure
- **Skills**: Competencies and expertise areas
- **History**: All interactions, meetings, feedback

## Adding a Team Member

### Quick Add
1. Navigate to **Circle** → **Team**
2. Click **+ Add Team Member**
3. Enter required fields:
   - First name
   - Last name
   - Email address
4. Click **Save**

### Full Profile Setup
For comprehensive tracking, also add:

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Job Title | Current role |
| Department | Organizational unit |
| Start Date | Tenure tracking |
| Location | Office/remote status |
| Phone | Contact info |
| Skills | Competency tracking |
| Notes | Personal context |

## Profile Photo

Add a photo to personalize the experience:
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG
- Recommended size: 200x200 pixels
- Falls back to initials if not set

## Skills & Competencies

Track what your team members are good at:

1. Open a team member's profile
2. Go to the **Skills** section
3. Add skills with proficiency levels:
   - Beginner
   - Intermediate
   - Advanced
   - Expert

Use skills to:
- Match people to projects
- Identify training needs
- Plan career development

## Viewing Team Member Details

Click any team member to see:

### Summary Tab
- Photo and basic info
- Quick stats (meetings held, tasks assigned)
- Recent activity

### 1:1 History
- All past and upcoming meetings
- Meeting notes and agenda items
- Recurring patterns

### Tasks & Projects
- Assigned tasks with status
- Project involvement
- Workload overview

### OKRs & Goals
- Individual OKRs they own
- Development goals
- Progress tracking

### Feedback History
- All feedback records
- Trends over time
- Recognition and coaching notes

## Bulk Operations

Select multiple team members for:
- Bulk email
- Export to CSV
- Mass status updates

## Archiving vs. Deleting

### Archive
- Keeps all history
- Removes from active lists
- Can be restored
- Use for: departures, transfers

### Delete
- Permanently removes
- Cannot be undone
- Use for: duplicate entries, test data

## Integration Points

Team members connect to:
- **1:1 Meetings**: Schedule and track
- **Tasks**: Assignment and ownership
- **Projects**: Team allocation
- **OKRs**: Individual objectives
- **KPIs**: Personal metrics
- **Feedback**: Performance records
- **Goals**: Development tracking

## Best Practices

1. **Keep profiles current** - Update titles and info promptly
2. **Add context** - Notes help you remember important details
3. **Track skills** - Enables better project matching
4. **Regular 1:1s** - Schedule recurring meetings for each member
5. **Complete profiles** - More data = better insights

## Privacy Considerations

- Employee data is stored locally by default
- Only you can see your team data
- Export carefully - contains personal information
- Consider data retention policies for departures
