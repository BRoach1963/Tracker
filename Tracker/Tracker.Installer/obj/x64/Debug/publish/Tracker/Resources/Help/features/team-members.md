# Team Members

The Team Members section allows you to manage your direct reports and track their information, performance, and development.

## Overview

Each team member profile contains:

- **Personal Information** - Name, email, phone number
- **Role & Position** - Job title, department, specialty
- **Employment Details** - Hire date, tenure
- **Profile Picture** - Visual identification

## Adding a Team Member

1. Navigate to **Team Members** in the sidebar
2. Click the **Add Team Member** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **First Name** (required)
   - **Last Name** (required)
   - **Email** (required)
   - **Role** - Select from predefined roles
   - **Hire Date** - When they joined the team

### Optional Fields

- **Phone** - Contact number
- **Job Title** - Their official title
- **Specialty** - Engineering specialty or focus area
- **Skill Level** - Junior, Mid, Senior, etc.
- **Profile Picture** - Upload a photo

## Editing Team Members

1. Double-click a team member row, or
2. Select a team member and click the **Edit** button
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

## Team Member Details View

When viewing a team member, you'll see tabs for:

### Details Tab
Basic information and contact details.

### Meetings Tab
History of all 1:1 meetings with this team member, including:
- Meeting dates
- Status (Completed, Scheduled, Cancelled)
- Key discussion points

### Feedback Tab
All feedback recorded for this team member:
- Positive recognition
- Constructive feedback
- Coaching notes

### Goals Tab
Individual development goals:
- Career goals
- Skill development
- Certifications in progress

## Team Member Statistics

The grid displays key metrics for each team member:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Last 1:1** | Date of the most recent 1:1 meeting |
| **Next 1:1** | Date of the next scheduled 1:1 |
| **Tasks** | Number of open tasks assigned |
| **Goals** | Number of active goals |
| **Status** | Active or Inactive |

## Best Practices

> 💡 Keep team member information up-to-date for accurate reporting.

> 💡 Add profile pictures to make the interface more personal.

> 💡 Review the Feedback and Goals tabs regularly to track development.
