# AI Help Bot

The AI Help Bot is your intelligent assistant that helps you use Tracker effectively and (in Pro tier) analyze your team data.

## Availability

| Plan | AI Help Bot | Data Analysis |
|------|:-----------:|:-------------:|
| Free | ❌ | ❌ |
| Standard | ✅ | ❌ |
| Pro | ✅ | ✅ |

## Opening the Help Bot

### From Any Screen
- Click the **✨** sparkle button (bottom-right corner)
- Or press **F1**

### From the Menu
- Click your profile icon → **Help**

## What Can the Help Bot Do?

### Help with Features (Standard+)

Ask about how to use Tracker:

> "How do I create an OKR?"

> "What's the difference between tasks and projects?"

> "How do I export my data?"

> "Where can I see my team's KPIs?"

> "How do I set up calendar sync?"

The bot knows all Tracker features and can guide you step-by-step.

### Best Practices (Standard+)

Get management advice:

> "How often should I have 1:1s?"

> "What makes a good OKR?"

> "How should I structure feedback?"

> "Tips for effective 1:1 agendas"

### Analyze Your Data (Pro Only)

Ask questions about your team:

> "Who has the most overdue tasks?"

> "What's our average OKR completion rate?"

> "Which team members haven't had a 1:1 in 30 days?"

> "Summarize John's feedback this quarter"

> "Show me KPIs that are off target"

**Note**: Data analysis requires the Pro plan. The bot will let you know if you ask a data question on a lower tier.

## Using the Help Bot

### Asking Questions

1. Type your question in the input box
2. Press **Enter** or click **Send**
3. Wait for the response (usually 2-5 seconds)

### Tips for Better Answers

**Be specific:**
- ❌ "How do tasks work?"
- ✅ "How do I assign a task to a team member?"

**Provide context:**
- ❌ "Why isn't it working?"
- ✅ "Why isn't my OKR showing progress from linked KPIs?"

**Ask follow-ups:**
- "Can you explain that differently?"
- "Give me an example"
- "What's the keyboard shortcut for that?"

### Conversation History

- The bot remembers your recent messages
- Ask follow-up questions naturally
- Click **Clear** to start fresh

### Refreshing Context

If the bot seems outdated:
1. Click **⟳ Refresh** 
2. This reloads your current data
3. Try your question again

## Privacy & Security

### What the Bot Can See
- Tracker documentation and features
- Your current data (team, tasks, etc.) - **Pro only**
- Current date and context

### What the Bot Cannot See
- Your password or authentication details
- Other users' data
- Deleted items
- External systems

### Data Handling
- Queries are processed by Google Gemini
- Minimal context is sent (only what's needed)
- No personal data is stored by the AI provider
- See our [Privacy Policy](https://pricklycactus.com/privacy)

## Troubleshooting

### "AI is not available"
- Check your subscription (requires Standard+)
- Verify internet connection
- AI may be temporarily disabled

### Slow Responses
- Normal response time: 2-5 seconds
- Longer for data analysis questions
- Check your internet connection

### "I don't have access to your data"
- Data analysis requires Pro plan
- Upgrade to Pro for data questions

### Incorrect Information
- AI can occasionally make mistakes
- Verify important information
- Report issues via **Report a Problem**
