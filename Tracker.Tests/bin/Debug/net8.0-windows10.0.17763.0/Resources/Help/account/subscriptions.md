## Free Plan - $0/month

Perfect for trying Tracker or managing a small team.

### What's Included
- **10 team members** maximum
- **Unlimited** 1:1 meetings, tasks, projects
- **Unlimited** OKRs, KPIs, goals, feedback
- **Quick notes** with rich formatting
- **8 beautiful themes**
- **Local database** storage
- **Community support** via forums
- **Fresh subscription** with each new account

### New Account Benefits
When you create a new account:
- You automatically start with a **fresh Free subscription**
- Your settings and preferences are completely independent
- No data or settings are shared with other accounts

### Limitations
- No AI Help Bot
- No reports or export
- No calendar sync
- 10 team member cap

### Best For
- Individual managers
- Small teams
- Trying before buying
- Personal use

## Standard Plan - $7/user/month

The sweet spot for most managers. Everything you need to run effective 1:1s and track team performance.

### What's Included
Everything in Free, plus:
- **100 team members**
- **AI Help Bot** - Get instant answers about app features
- **Basic Reports** - Team summaries, meeting history
- **Calendar Sync** - Google Calendar and Outlook integration
- **Email Support** - Response within 24 hours

### AI Help Bot (Standard)
In Standard, the AI can help you:
- Learn how to use features
- Find information in the app
- Get best practices for 1:1s and OKRs
- Troubleshoot issues

**Not included**: AI cannot analyze your team data in Standard tier.

### Best For
- Growing teams (10-100 members)
- Managers who want calendar integration
- Those who value AI assistance
- Teams needing basic reporting

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change plans anytime?
Yes! Upgrade or downgrade at any time from Account Settings. Changes take effect immediately.

### What happens when I downgrade?
- Your data is preserved
- Features from higher tier become unavailable
- If you exceed team member limits, you can view but not add more

### Is there a trial for paid plans?
Yes! Start a 14-day free trial of any paid plan. No credit card required.

### What payment methods do you accept?
- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- PayPal
- Invoice (Enterprise only)

### Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. No long-term contracts. Cancel from Account Settings and your access continues until the end of your billing period.

### Do you offer team/volume discounts?
Yes, for teams of 50+ users. Contact sales@pricklycactus.com for custom pricing.

### Is there a nonprofit discount?
Yes! Nonprofits receive 25% off any paid plan. Contact support with proof of nonprofit status.

### What about students/educators?
Students and educators receive 50% off. Verify with your .edu email or school ID.

## Upgrading Your Plan

### From the App
1. Click your **profile icon** (top-right)
2. Select **Account**
3. Click **Upgrade Plan**
4. Choose your new plan
5. Enter payment information
6. Confirm upgrade

### Features Activate Instantly
- AI Help Bot: Available immediately
- Calendar Sync: Set up in Settings
- Reports: Find in Chronicle section
